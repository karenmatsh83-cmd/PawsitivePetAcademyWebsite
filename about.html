About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Learn more about our purpose and passion for animals.

Who We Are

Pawsitive Pet Academy is an educational initiative focused on pet care, animal welfare and responsible pet ownership. We aim to help learners develop knowledge and practical skills for caring for animals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage responsible animal care by providing accessible learning opportunities in pet grooming, training, animal behaviour and general pet welfare.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a community of knowledgeable, confident and compassionate people who understand the importance of animal welfare.

Our Values

  • Kindness and compassion towards animals.
  • Responsible pet ownership.
  • Respect and patience.
  • Continuous learning and improvement.
  • Commitment to animal welfare.
